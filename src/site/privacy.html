Legal & Privacy

GoFully Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

GoFully is a browser extension designed to help you capture, edit, protect, extract text from, and export content from webpages.

Your privacy is important to us. GoFully is designed around a simple principle: your captured content should remain under your control.

This Privacy Policy explains what information GoFully accesses, how it is used, where it is processed, and what information we do and do not collect.

Core Privacy Principle
GoFully executes capture, image redaction, OCR text extraction, and PDF generation locally within your browser sandbox. We do not transmit or monetize your captures.

1. Information GoFully Processes

GoFully may process the following information when you actively use its features:

Webpage content

When you choose a capture mode, GoFully may access the visible webpage or the portion of the webpage required to perform the requested capture. Depending on the feature you select, this may include:

  • Text displayed on the webpage
  • Images and visual elements
  • Page layout and dimensions
  • Content within the selected capture area
  • Content required to create a full-page or scrolling screenshot

GoFully accesses this information only to perform the capture action requested by you.

OCR content

When you use Capture Text (OCR), GoFully processes the selected image or webpage area to recognize text. The resulting text is provided to you so that you can review or copy it.

Captured images

When you create a screenshot, the resulting image is processed by GoFully for features such as:

  • Annotation (shapes, arrows, callouts, text)
  • Cropping
  • Highlighting
  • Blur
  • Pixelation
  • Redaction
  • Export (PNG, PDF)

User settings

GoFully may store extension preferences required to remember your configuration (such as default export format or PDF page size) and provide the expected functionality.

2. How GoFully Uses This Information

GoFully uses accessed content only to provide the features you explicitly request. For example:

  • Full Page Capture uses webpage content to create a complete screenshot.
  • Visible Area Capture captures the currently visible portion of the page.
  • Selected Area Capture captures the area you select.
  • Scrolling Capture captures content while scrolling through the selected area.
  • OCR processes captured content to extract text.
  • Annotation tools modify captured images.
  • Blur, Pixelate, and Redact tools modify sensitive areas before you share or export them.
  • Export features create the file format you select.

GoFully does not use captured webpage content for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

3. Local Processing

GoFully performs all capture and editing functionality entirely within your browser. Captured screenshots, OCR results, annotations, and exported files are never uploaded to any GoFully server. There are no GoFully servers that receive your content.

All image processing, blur effects, text recognition (via local WebAssembly), and PDF generation run inside your browser's sandboxed environment. Your data does not leave your device.

If this architecture changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and notify users before any such change takes effect.

4. Data Sharing

GoFully does not sell captured webpage content, screenshots, or OCR results. GoFully does not share captured content with advertisers or data brokers.

Captured content is used solely to provide the feature you intentionally activate. If you choose to copy, save, export, or otherwise share a captured result, that action is controlled by you.

5. Website Access

GoFully may require access to webpage content to perform its core screenshot and OCR functionality. This access is necessary because the extension cannot capture or extract content from webpages without interacting with the webpage being captured.

GoFully does not use this access to continuously monitor your browsing activity for advertising or profiling purposes.

6. Data Storage

GoFully does not require you to create an account to use its core functionality.

Captured screenshots and OCR results are handled as part of the feature you initiate. If you save or export a file, the file is saved to the destination you choose through the browser or operating system.

7. Third-Party Services

GoFully's core capture and editing functionality does not require sending captured webpage content to third-party services.

If third-party services are introduced for analytics, payments, crash reporting, cloud storage, or other functionality, this policy will be updated to describe what information those services receive and why.

8. Children's Privacy

GoFully is a general-purpose browser extension and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through GoFully.

9. Security

GoFully is designed to minimize unnecessary data access and processing. We follow a least-privilege approach and request only permissions required for the extension's functionality.

We also recommend that users review captured content before sharing it, particularly when screenshots contain passwords, authentication information, financial information, personal communications, or other sensitive information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when GoFully's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
