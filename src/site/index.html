How It Works

Up and running in four simple steps

GoFully is built for speed and clarity. Go from webpage capture to polished, redacted exports without leaving your active tab.

Install the extension

Add GoFully to Chrome in under 10 seconds. No account, login, or credit card required. The extension is immediately active.

Choose your capture mode

Select Full Page (Alt+Shift+F on Windows / Option+Shift+F on Mac), Visible Area (Option+Shift+V), Custom Selection, or Scrolling Area to capture exactly what you need.

Annotate, redact, or extract text

Add CleanShot-style curved arrows, step bubbles (1, 2, 3), blur out sensitive credentials, or extract text with on-device WebAssembly OCR.

Export with confidence

Copy directly to your clipboard or download in crisp 4K UHD PNG, JPG, or auto-paginated multi-page PDF — 100% processed in your browser.
