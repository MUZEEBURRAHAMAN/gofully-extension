Common Troubleshooting Guides

Step-by-step solutions for frequent questions and capture scenarios.

Why can't GoFully capture a particular website?

Some websites use cross-origin iframes, dynamic infinite loaders, or canvas-based layout engines that may lock standard window scroll events.
  • Try using Visible Area (`Alt+Shift+V` / `⌥⇧V`) or Selected Area to capture the section directly.
  • Verify the webpage is not a restricted internal page (e.g., `chrome://extensions` or the Chrome Web Store).

Why does Full Page Capture look different from the live webpage?

Sticky headers, animated banners, and lazy-loading media can shift position when scrolled programmatically. GoFully includes automated sticky header management and pre-scroll triggers to mitigate this, but for heavy single-page apps (SPAs), using Scrolling Area gives you full control over the stitched area.

Why is OCR text extraction inaccurate on low-resolution images?

Optical Character Recognition works best when text has high contrast against its background and is at least 14px in size.
  • Zoom in on the webpage (`Ctrl +` or `Cmd +`) before taking the OCR selection to provide more pixel clarity to the OCR engine.
  • Avoid selecting heavy decorative handwriting fonts or blurred watermarks.
