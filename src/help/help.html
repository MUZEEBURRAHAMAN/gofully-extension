Help

Learn how to capture full pages, extract text with OCR, annotate, and export.

Capture an entire webpage from top to bottom, including all content below the fold:

  1. Open the webpage you want to capture.
  2. Click the GoFully extension icon and select "Full Page" (or press shortcut Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+F).
  3. GoFully automatically manages sticky navigation bars, pre-triggers lazy-loaded media, and produces a pixel-perfect, seamless capture.
  4. The captured screenshot opens in the in-page action bar and annotation studio for instant editing or saving.
Pro Tip: GoFully scrolls the page, captures each screen and checks every seam. If two pieces do not line up, it captures that part again.

Instantly grab whatever is currently in your viewport without scrolling:

  1. Click the GoFully icon and click "Visible Area" (or press Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+V).
  2. The capture appears in a card at the top right of the page, ready to copy, save, edit or pin. See Recent captures below, or choose a different action in Settings.

Drag to frame any rectangular section of the screen:

  1. Click the GoFully icon and select "Selected Area".
  2. Click and drag on the webpage to define your capture area. Live pixel dimensions and resize handles appear automatically.
  3. Click "Capture" (or press Enter) to finish, or Esc to cancel.

Capture a long scrollable column (like a chat panel, article body, or feed) without grabbing the whole browser width:

  1. Select "Scrolling Area" from the popup.
  2. Draw a box around the area you want to scroll.
  3. Click "Auto-Scroll" with your desired speed (Slow / Med / Fast), or use "Manual Capture" to grab frame-by-frame.
  4. GoFully stitches the vertical frames into a single tall image.

Extract unselectable text from images, locked documents, graphs, and video streams:

  1. Select "Capture Text (OCR)" in the GoFully popup.
  2. Drag a selection box over the text on your screen.
  3. The text is recognized locally with WebAssembly OCR and automatically copied to your clipboard.
Privacy Note: All OCR processing runs 100% locally in your browser. Zero text or images ever leave your computer.

Capture one card, table, chart or chat message without dragging a box around it.

  1. Click the GoFully icon and choose "Capture one element".
  2. Move the pointer over the page. A blue outline follows the element under it and shows its size.
  3. Press ↑ to select the parent element or ↓ to step back down.
  4. Click to capture. Press Esc to cancel.
Tall elements: GoFully scrolls the element to the middle of the screen and captures the part you can see. Use Full Page or Scrolling Area when you need the rest.

Capture a menu, a tooltip or a hover state that closes the moment you click somewhere else.

  1. In the popup, set Delay (next to "More ways to capture") to 3, 5 or 10 seconds.
  2. Click Full page or Visible area. The popup closes and a countdown appears at the top of the page.
  3. Open the menu or hover the element you want in the shot. GoFully captures when the countdown reaches zero.
  • Press Esc to cancel the countdown.
  • The countdown never appears in the image.
  • The same setting is in Settings as Delay before capture. Set it back to Off for instant captures.

Mark up a live website, then capture it with your marks in place.

  1. Click "Draw on Page" in the popup or press Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+H.
  2. Pick a tool from the bar at the bottom of the page and drag to draw.
  3. Click Visible or Full page in the bar to capture. GoFully hides the bar first, so it never appears in the image.
  4. Press Esc to finish.
  • Tools: Pen P, Highlighter H, Highlight text T, Arrow A, Rectangle R, Ellipse O, Text X, Sticky note N, Eraser E. Press V to use the page normally.
  • Highlight text: Select words on the page and GoFully paints a marker over them.
  • Undo and redo: Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z.
Your marks stay: GoFully saves them for each page address on this device. Open Draw on Page there again and they come back. The trash button clears them.

Marks sit at fixed positions on the page. They do not follow content that scrolls inside its own box, which some web apps use.

Hide private details on the live page, then capture it. This works at any length, including full-page captures, because the covers are part of the page while you capture.

  1. Click Redact page in the popup. GoFully covers emails, card numbers, phone numbers, ID numbers, IBANs, IP addresses and API keys in the page text and in form fields.
  2. Check the bar at the bottom: it lists what was hidden, for example "Hidden: 2 emails, 1 card number".
  3. Click any text or element to hide it too. Click a cover to show what is under it again. To hide part of a sentence, select it and press H.
  4. Choose Black boxes or Blur, then click Capture visible or Capture full page.
  5. Click Done or press Esc. The page goes back exactly as it was.
  • Text that loads while Redact page is on, such as a lazy section or the next page of a feed, is covered as it appears.
  • Captures from the popup or a shortcut also include the covers while Redact page is on.
  • Show all removes every cover without leaving the mode.
Check before you share: GoFully finds common patterns, not every private detail. Names and addresses are not found automatically, so click them to hide them. Black boxes hide text completely; blur can sometimes be read back on short words.

The matching runs on the page itself. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Check spacing and colors on a live page, in the same CSS pixels the code uses.

  1. Click Measure in the popup.
  2. Hover any element. A card shows what it is (Button, Heading, Image and so on), its name in the code, its size, and its padding, margin and text style. Green bands on the page show padding and amber bands show margin.
  3. Click an element to select it, then hover another one. Red lines show the gap between the two in pixels.
  4. Drag anywhere for a free ruler. It shows the length and how far it goes across and down.
  5. Press C or click Pick color, then click anywhere on screen. The hex value is copied and the color is added to the bar. Click a saved color to copy its hex again, or Shift-click it for RGB.
  • Press Esc once to let go of the selected element, and again to leave Measure.
  • Capture while Measure is on and the measurements stay in the image, which is handy for design feedback. The bar at the bottom is left out.

Visible area, selected area, element and delayed captures appear in a stack at the top right of the page.

  • The newest capture is open with Copy, PNG, JPG, WebP, PDF, Edit, Pin and Guide. Earlier ones fold into rows: click a row to open it, or use its copy button.
  • Each card acts on its own capture, so you can take several in a row and deal with them afterwards.
  • Drag a preview onto your desktop or into a folder to save the image there. Apps that accept dropped files take it too.
  • After a few seconds without use, the stack tucks into a small tab at the right edge. Click it to bring the stack back, or click Hide to tuck it away now. Clear all removes it.
  • The stack never appears in your next capture.

To skip the card, open Settings and choose what happens in After a capture: copy to the clipboard, save as PNG or PDF, open the editor, or pin the image on the page. Full-page captures always open in a new tab.

GoFully reads QR codes and common barcodes in your captures, on your device.

  • On the capture card: codes found in a capture are listed on its card, with Copy. Folded rows show a small QR tag.
  • In the editor: click Find codes in the top bar to list every code in the image, including long full-page captures.
  • Links: only web addresses (http and https) get an Open button, and they open in a new tab. Anything else, such as Wi-Fi details or plain text, can only be copied.

If a code is not found, zoom the page so the code is larger, then capture again.

Open the built-in editor to polish your screenshots before sharing:

  • Straight & Curved Arrows: Click and drag to point out elements; use the dropdown to switch to curved arrows.
  • Callout Speech Bubbles: Add editable callout notes.
  • Step Numbers: Click to place auto-incrementing numbered badges (1, 2, 3...) for reproduction steps.
  • Spotlight Mode: Darkens the page around a focal box to draw attention.
  • Glass Blur & Redaction: Protect private info using Glass Smooth Blur, Mosaic Pixelate, or Blackout redaction.
  • Auto-redact: Open the arrow next to the Blur tool and choose Auto-redact sensitive info. GoFully reads the screenshot on your device and covers emails, card numbers, phone numbers, ID numbers, IP addresses and API keys with black boxes. Check each box, then move or delete any you do not want. Undo removes them all.
  • Cropping Studio: Press X to open the cropper. Pick Free, Original, 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 9:16 or other presets, and nudge the box with the arrow keys. Your annotations move with the image and stay editable. Undo and redo bring back the previous size.

Turn a plain screenshot into an image you can post. Click Beautify in the editor’s top bar.

  • Backgrounds: Choose a gradient or pattern, one of eight built-in wallpapers, or your own image with + Your image.
  • Layout: Set padding, corner radius, shadow, aspect ratio and a window frame. With an aspect ratio on, the nine-point grid decides where the screenshot sits in the extra space.
  • Border: Pick a width and a colour.
  • Watermark: Type your handle or site, then set size, opacity, colour and position.
  • 3D mockup: Tilt X, Tilt Y, Rotate, Zoom and Pan tilt the screenshot in perspective. Six presets give you a starting point, from Flat to Hero.
  • Progressive blur: Blur grows toward one edge, or away from the centre. Apply it to the screenshot only or to the whole scene.
  • Collage: Add more images and lay them out side by side or stacked.

Share your look

Save stores your settings as a preset. Export presets writes a .gofullypreset file. A teammate clicks Import and gets the same presets. A custom background image stays on your device and does not travel inside a preset.

Export your finished screenshots with high DPI preservation:

  • Copy to Clipboard: Instant PNG copy ready to paste into Slack, Jira, Figma, Notion, or email.
  • Save PNG: Export in Native Resolution, 1080p HD, or 4K UHD.
  • Save PDF: Automatically paginated across clean A4 pages with crisp high-resolution rendering.

Combine several captures into one how-to document.

  1. Capture the first screen. In the editor, open Export and choose Add to guide. On the capture card, click Guide.
  2. Repeat for each screen.
  3. Click the list icon in the popup to open your guide.
  4. Name the guide and write a line under each step. Use the arrows to reorder steps and the cross to delete one.
  5. Click Export PDF, or Markdown + images for a ZIP with guide.md and one JPG per step.

A guide holds up to 30 steps. GoFully stores it on this device. Clear removes it.

Keep a reference image on screen while you work.

  • Click Pin on the capture card, or choose Export, Pin to page in the editor.
  • Drag the image to move it and drag its corner to resize it.
  • Hover over it to show the opacity slider and the close button.

You can keep five pins on a page. They stay until you close them or reload the tab. GoFully does not save them.

History keeps your last 50 captures. Click the grid icon in the popup and type in the search box.

  • What it searches: the words inside the screenshot, the page title and the page address. Type several words to narrow the list.
  • How it works: GoFully reads the text of each new capture on your device and stores it next to the thumbnail. On tall pages it reads the top.
  • Opening one: Click a result to open the page it came from. With Keep recent captures on in Settings, History stores the full image of your last 10 captures on this device, and a click reopens that capture in the editor. With it off, History keeps thumbnails only. Save or pin a capture when you need the original.
  • Older captures: Captures made before this version have no text to search. Their titles and addresses still match.

To stop GoFully reading new captures, turn off Search inside screenshots in Settings. Clear All deletes thumbnails and text together.

Accelerate your workflow with global and editor hotkeys:

  • Alt+S — Open GoFully popup
  • Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+F — Capture full page
  • Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+V — Capture visible area
  • Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+A — Capture selected area
  • Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+H — Draw on this page (pen, highlighter, arrows, text, sticky notes) — Esc to finish. Assign a Chrome-wide key at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  • Measure: C picks a color, Esc lets go of the selected element, then leaves. Redact page: H hides the selected text, Esc restores the page and leaves. Delay: Esc cancels the countdown.
  • V — Select tool | A — Arrow | R — Rectangle | T — Text
  • B — Blur/Redact | N — Step Number | S — Spotlight | X — Crop
  • Settings lists the key Chrome has bound to each command. If another extension already used one of your keys, Settings says so, and Change in Chrome opens the page where you pick a new one.
  • ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z — Undo | ⌘Y / Ctrl+Y — Redo

GoFully is built from the ground up to guarantee user privacy:

  • 100% Offline Processing: All stitching, cropping, blur filters, and OCR run locally on your device.
  • Zero Data Exfiltration: No screenshots or text are ever transmitted to external servers.
  • No Tracking: Zero analytics or telemetry SDKs included.
  • Local reading: Auto-redact and History search read your screenshots with an OCR engine bundled in the extension. The text stays in your browser's storage.
  • Local storage only: Guides, saved marks and presets live in your browser and leave only when you export them.
  • On the page, not online: Redact page, Measure and QR code reading run on your device. Redact page changes the page only while it is on, and Done restores it.
