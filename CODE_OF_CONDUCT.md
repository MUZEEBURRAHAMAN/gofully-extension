# Code of Conduct

GoFully is a small project that people use for ordinary work, and contributors should feel at ease helping with it. These few rules apply in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is discussed.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Keep feedback specific and about the work.
- Welcome newcomers. Explain jargon and point to the right place when someone asks in the wrong one.
- Respect privacy. Do not share other people's personal information, and do not paste real customer data, keys or screenshots with private details into issues.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments, or demeaning language about who someone is.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a request after a maintainer has answered it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. Serious or repeated problems may lead to a permanent ban from the project.
