**Category:** Productivity. **Language:** English.

**Summary line for the top of the description** (already in `description.txt`):
GoFully is a free full page screenshot extension for Chrome. Capture a whole page, one element or a scrolling panel. Draw on a live site, hide sensitive text before you capture, measure spacing and colors, make the image presentable, and export it. Everything runs on your device. No account, no uploads.

### Single purpose
Capture web page screenshots and make them ready to share: full-page and partial capture, on-device text reading, annotation and redaction, and export.

### Permission justifications (unchanged from 1.1.2)
- **activeTab**: capture the tab the person is looking at, only after they ask.
- **tabs**: read the title and address of the captured tab for history and file names, and open the editor and review pages.
- **scripting**: scroll the page and hide fixed headers during a full-page capture, and inject the on-page tools (Draw on Page, Redact page, Measure, element picker).
- **offscreen**: stitch screenshots, and run text and QR code reading in a hidden extension page.
- **downloads**: save PNG, JPG, WebP, PDF and Markdown files when the person chooses Save.
- **storage**: keep settings, history, guides and saved marks on this device.
- **Host access (all sites)**: a screenshot tool has to work on whatever page the person is on. Nothing is read until they start a capture.

### Data usage declaration
GoFully sends no user data off the device and loads no remote code. History, guides, saved marks and settings stay in the browser's own storage. Redact page, Measure, text reading and QR reading all run locally. Declare that you do not collect or transmit user data.

### Search terms

## What to say in the update notes / announcement

**GoFully 1.1.3: the biggest update yet**

- **Redact page**: cover emails, card numbers, phone numbers, ID numbers and API keys on the live page before you capture, even on long pages. Click anything else to hide it.
- **Measure**: sizes, padding, margin, gaps and a free ruler on any page, plus a color picker that copies hex.
- **Delayed capture**: wait 3, 5 or 10 seconds, open the menu or hover the tooltip, and GoFully captures it.
- **Capture stack**: recent captures stay in the corner with Copy, Save, Edit and Pin on each. Drag one onto your desktop or into a folder.
- **QR codes and barcodes** in a capture are read on your device.
- **Draw on Page, Capture Element, Beautify, Auto-redact, step-by-step guides and history search** are new too.
- Still free, still offline, still no account. No new permissions.
