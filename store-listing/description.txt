GoFully is a free full page screenshot extension for Chrome. Capture a whole page, one element or a scrolling panel. Draw on a live site, hide sensitive text, make the image presentable, and export it. Everything runs on your device. No account, no uploads.

Annotate with arrows and callouts. Auto-redact emails, card numbers and API keys. Pull text out of images with on-device OCR. Add wallpapers, 3D mockups and watermarks. Export to PNG, JPG, WebP or PDF, or string several captures into a step-by-step guide.

📸 7 WAYS TO CAPTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. FULL PAGE SCREENSHOT
Captures the entire scrollable page from top to bottom. GoFully scrolls the page, captures each screen and checks every seam, and captures a piece again when two pieces do not line up. Sticky headers and lazy-loaded images are handled for you.

2. VISIBLE VIEWPORT
One-click instant capture of exactly what is visible on your screen.

3. SELECTED REGION
Click and drag to select any custom area on the page with live pixel dimension indicators and movable handles.

4. SCROLLING AREA CAPTURE
Draw a boundary around any fixed section of a webpage (such as an article column or dashboard panel). GoFully automatically scrolls within that region and stitches the frames together into a single tall image with duplicate detection.

5. CAPTURE TEXT (ON-DEVICE OCR)
Select any area containing text—including photos, scanned PDFs, diagrams, and video frames—to extract and copy editable text directly to your clipboard using on-device optical character recognition.

6. CAPTURE ELEMENT
Hover over the page and a blue outline follows the element under your pointer. Press the arrow keys to pick its parent or child, then click. Cards, tables and chat messages come out cropped to the pixel.

7. DRAW ON PAGE
Mark up a live website before you capture it. Pen, highlighter, text highlight, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, text and sticky notes, with undo and redo. Capture the visible area or the full page with your marks in place. GoFully saves your marks per page address on your device, and they come back when you reopen Draw on Page there.

• Arrows & Lines: Straight arrows, curved arrows, and line tools
• Shapes: Rectangle, Square, Circle, Ellipse, Triangle, and Polygon — one Shape tool, six options
• Callouts & Notes: Speech bubbles with customizable text
• Step Numbers: Auto-incrementing badges (1, 2, 3...) for reproduction steps and tutorials
• Spotlight Mode: Dim surrounding areas to focus attention on key UI components
• Freehand Drawing: Pen tool with adjustable stroke width
• Cropping Studio: Preset aspect ratios (Free, 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 9:16, 21:9, A4, Twitter, OG Image) with grid guides and keyboard nudging. Your annotations move with the crop and stay editable
• Zoom up to 1000%: Fine-tune annotations pixel-by-pixel on large or full-page captures
• Undo / Redo History: Multi-step history stack for seamless editing
• Curated Palette: 8 preset color swatches with stroke width adjustment

Protect private information before sharing screenshots:
• Glass Smooth Blur: Soft Gaussian-style blur for sensitive text or numbers
• Mosaic Pixelate: Pixelation filter for avatars or user IDs
• Redact Blackout: Solid black box redaction for credentials and private data
• Auto-redact: One click finds emails, card numbers, phone numbers, ID numbers, IP addresses and API keys, and covers each with a black box. Review every box before you export

Global
• Alt+S — Open GoFully popup
• Ctrl+Shift+F (Cmd+Shift+F on Mac) — Capture full page
• Ctrl+Shift+V (Cmd+Shift+V on Mac) — Capture visible area
• Ctrl+Shift+A (Cmd+Shift+A on Mac) — Capture selected area
• Ctrl+Shift+H (Cmd+Shift+H on Mac) — Draw on page
• Chrome allows four default shortcuts per extension, so GoFully handles the rest on the page. Settings lists the keys Chrome has bound and flags any another extension took
• Change any shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts

Editor
V Select · A Arrow · R Rectangle · E Ellipse · C Callout · L Line
P Pen · T Text · S Spotlight · B Blur · N Step · X Crop · H Highlight
• Enter — Apply crop
• Esc — Cancel / Deselect
• Cmd/Ctrl+Z — Undo · Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z or Cmd/Ctrl+Y — Redo
• Cmd/Ctrl+D — Duplicate selected object
• Cmd/Ctrl+= / Cmd/Ctrl+- — Zoom in / out
• Delete or Backspace — Remove selected object

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: Is GoFully free?
A: Yes. Every capture mode, the full annotation editor, OCR, and PDF export are free — no account, subscription, or paywalled features.

Q: Does GoFully work offline?
A: Yes. Captures, annotations, and OCR all run 100% on-device in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Q: Can GoFully capture a full page without cutting anything off?
A: Yes. Full Page Screenshot scrolls and stitches the entire page, including pages with sticky headers, lazy-loaded images, and nested or shadow-DOM scroll containers.

Q: Can I extract text from a screenshot or image?
A: Yes. GoFully includes on-device OCR — select any area of text, a photo, a scanned PDF, or a video frame, and copy the extracted text to your clipboard.

Q: What image and document formats can I export to?
A: PNG, JPG, WebP (with an adjustable Image Quality setting), and auto-paginated PDF, at Native, 1080p HD, or 4K UHD resolution.

Q: Is GoFully a good GoFullPage, FireShot, Awesome Screenshot, or Nimbus alternative?
A: Yes — GoFully covers everything those do (full page, visible area, PDF export) plus scrolling-area capture, a free annotation editor with blur/redaction, and local OCR, without an account or paywall.

Q: Does GoFully save a history of my screenshots?
A: Yes. The popup keeps a thumbnail grid of your last 50 captures with source site and timestamp. You can search by the words inside each screenshot, and reopen recent ones in the editor if you turn on Keep recent captures. History stays on your device and you can switch the text search off in Settings.

Q: Can I annotate a live website?
A: Yes. Draw on Page adds a toolbar to the site. Mark it up, then capture the visible area or the full page with your marks included.

Q: Does Auto-redact catch everything?
A: It finds emails, card numbers, phone numbers, ID numbers, IP addresses and API keys that OCR can read. Check each black box before you share. Text that OCR misses stays visible.
