GoFully is a complete browser screenshot and screen capture extension for capturing full pages, visible viewports, custom selected areas, scrolling regions, and extracting text from images (OCR) with built-in annotation and privacy redaction.

Capture entire scrollable webpages, select scrolling areas, extract text from web images, annotate with arrows and callout notes, blur sensitive details, and export to PNG or auto-paginated PDF files—all processed locally on your device.

📸 5 CAPTURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. FULL PAGE SCREENSHOT
Captures the entire scrollable page from top to bottom. Integrates with the Chrome DevTools Protocol for single-shot captures on standard pages to eliminate stitching seams, with automatic scroll-stitch fallback for long documents, smart sticky header handling, and lazy-load image pre-scrolling.

2. VISIBLE VIEWPORT
One-click instant capture of exactly what is visible on your screen.

3. SELECTED REGION
Click and drag to select any custom area on the page with live pixel dimension indicators and movable handles.

4. SCROLLING AREA CAPTURE
Draw a boundary around any fixed section of a webpage (such as an article column or dashboard panel). GoFully automatically scrolls within that region and stitches the frames together into a single tall image with duplicate detection.

5. CAPTURE TEXT (ON-DEVICE OCR)
Select any area containing text—including photos, scanned PDFs, diagrams, and video frames—to extract and copy editable text directly to your clipboard using on-device optical character recognition.

Annotate and organize visual feedback directly in the browser:
• Arrows & Lines: Straight arrows, curved arrows, and line tools
• Shapes: Rectangles and ellipses
• Callouts & Notes: Speech bubbles with customizable text
• Step Numbers: Auto-incrementing badges (1, 2, 3...) for reproduction steps and tutorials
• Spotlight Mode: Dim surrounding areas to focus attention on key UI components
• Freehand Drawing: Pen tool with adjustable stroke width
• Cropping Studio: Preset aspect ratios (Freeform, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:2, 2:1) with grid guides and keyboard nudging
• Undo / Redo History: Multi-step history stack for seamless editing
• Curated Palette: 8 preset color swatches with stroke width adjustment

🔒 PRIVACY REDACTION: BLUR, PIXELATE & BLACKOUT

Protect private information before sharing screenshots:
• Glass Smooth Blur: Soft Gaussian-style blur for sensitive text or numbers
• Mosaic Pixelate: Pixelation filter for avatars or user IDs
• Redact Blackout: Solid black box redaction for credentials and private data
