SnapForge — the most powerful free screenshot extension for Chrome. 5 capture modes, built-in editor, PDF export. 100% offline. Zero permissions beyond the active tab.

One click. Full page. Done.

Click the SnapForge icon (or press Alt+S) and pick your capture mode. Your screenshot is ready in seconds — copy to clipboard, save as PNG, or download as auto-paginated PDF. No account needed. No data leaves your device. Ever.

5 CAPTURE MODES — MORE THAN ANY FREE EXTENSION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. FULL PAGE SCREENSHOT
Captures the entire page from top to bottom. Uses Chrome DevTools Protocol for pixel-perfect single-shot capture on pages under 16,384px — no stitching artifacts. Automatically falls back to intelligent scroll-and-stitch for longer pages with smart sticky header detection and lazy-load pre-scrolling.

2. VISIBLE AREA
One-click capture of exactly what you see. Perfect for quick grabs.

3. SELECTED AREA
Drag to select any region on the page. Dashed selection box with live dimensions badge shows exactly what you're capturing.

4. SCROLLING AREA CAPTURE (like CleanShot — but free!)
Draw a rectangle around any part of the page (e.g., just the article column, ignoring the sidebar). SnapForge auto-scrolls within that region and stitches every frame into one tall image. Choose Auto-Scroll with speed control (slow / medium / fast) or Manual mode for tricky infinite-scroll pages. Duplicate frame detection automatically stops at the bottom.

5. SCROLLABLE ELEMENT CAPTURE
The feature no other extension offers. Hover over any nested scrollable container — chat boxes, code blocks, log panels, data tables, API response viewers — and click to capture its FULL scrollable content as a single image. Green overlay highlights all scrollable elements on the page.
